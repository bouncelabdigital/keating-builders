Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Who we are

Keating Builders is a construction company based in Berkshire County, Massachusetts. This policy covers our website at keatingbuilders.com.

What we collect

The only personal information we collect through this website is what you choose to send us through our contact form:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if you provide it
  • The type of project you're interested in
  • Any details you include in your message

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing purposes.

How submissions are processed

Contact form submissions are delivered to us by email through Web3Forms, a form processing service. Web3Forms handles your submission according to its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to respond to you and, if we work together, to carry out your project. This website does not store form submissions in a database.

Your data

Because this website does not keep a database of form submissions, there is nothing stored on the site itself to access or delete.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
