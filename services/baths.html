Our Approach

Every Detail.
Every Surface.

A great bathroom remodel is defined by what you can't easily see: proper waterproofing, solid substrate, precise tile layout, and fixtures set correctly the first time. These are the details that separate a bathroom that looks good on day one from one that looks good a decade from now.

Keating Builders handles full bathroom renovations at every scale — from a half-bath refresh to a complete master suite overhaul. We do the demolition, the waterproofing, the tile work, the custom vanity installation, the plumbing, the glass, and the final detailing. One crew, one standard of quality throughout.

We also offer ADA and accessibility modifications for homeowners planning for the long term — grab bars, curbless showers, comfort-height fixtures, and wider doorways all handled with the same craftsmanship as our luxury builds.

How We Work

Our Remodel Process

Six clear phases from design to completion — with quality checks at every step.

  1. 1

    Design Consultation

    We assess your bathroom, discuss your goals, and develop a design and budget tailored to your space.

  2. 2

    Selections

    Tile, fixtures, vanity, glass, and hardware — chosen with our guidance and confirmed before work begins.

  3. 3

    Demolition

    Clean removal of existing finishes, with full protection for adjacent areas and careful waste disposal.

  4. 4

    Rough-In

    Waterproofing membrane, plumbing, electrical, and substrate work — the foundation everything else rests on.

  5. 5

    Tile & Finishes

    Precision tile setting, vanity and fixture installation, glass work, and all surface finishes applied.

  6. 6

    Completion

    Final detailing, caulking, hardware, and a thorough walkthrough to confirm everything meets our standard.
