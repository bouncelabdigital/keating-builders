How We Work

Our Renovation Process

A structured process that keeps your kitchen project on time, on budget, and your household minimally disrupted.

  1. 1

    Design Consultation

    We walk your space, discuss your goals, and develop an initial design concept and budget range.

  2. 2

    Material Selection

    Choose cabinets, countertops, tile, hardware, and fixtures with our guidance and trade pricing.

  3. 3

    Demolition

    We remove the old kitchen cleanly and efficiently, with full protection for adjacent living areas.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Rough-in, cabinetry, countertops, tile, plumbing, and electrical — installed by our own crew to our standards.

  5. 5

    Finishing

    Painting, trim, hardware, appliances, and a thorough punch list walkthrough to close out the project.
