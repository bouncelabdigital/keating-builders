Our Approach

From Gut to Gorgeous. Custom Start to Finish.

A kitchen renovation is more than new cabinets. It's a complete reimagining of how you live in your home. Keating Builders handles full kitchen renovations from the first demo swing through the final punch list walkthrough, no piecemeal project management required.

We're specialists in custom cabinetry. Every cabinet we design and install is built for your exact space and your exact needs, no off-the-shelf compromise. Pair that with precision countertop fabrication, expert tile work, and careful layout optimization and the result is a kitchen that functions as beautifully as it looks.

Our established trade relationships mean we can source premium materials at better pricing than most homeowners can access on their own. And our scheduling approach is designed to minimize the disruption to your home, because we know living through a renovation is hard enough.

Our Renovation Process

A structured process that keeps your kitchen project on time, on budget, and your household minimally disrupted.

  1. 01

    Design Consultation

    We walk your space, discuss your goals, and develop an initial design concept and budget range.

  2. 02

    Material Selection

    Choose cabinets, countertops, tile, hardware, and fixtures with our guidance and trade pricing.

  3. 03

    Demolition

    We remove the old kitchen cleanly and efficiently, with full protection for adjacent living areas.

  4. 04

    Installation

    Rough-in, cabinetry, countertops, tile, plumbing, and electrical, installed by our own crew to our standards.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Painting, trim, hardware, appliances, and a thorough punch list walkthrough to close out the project.
