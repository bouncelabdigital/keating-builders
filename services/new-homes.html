Our Approach

Your Vision. Our Expertise.

Building a custom home is one of the most significant investments you'll ever make. At Keating Builders, we treat every new home as a personal project, not a production build. We work directly with you and your architect from the earliest planning stages through the day you receive your keys.

Our team has deep roots in Berkshire County. We understand the land, the climate, the local permit process, and the trades. That local knowledge translates into fewer surprises and a smoother build for our clients.

Every new home we build is a fully turnkey project. That means one point of contact, one transparent contract, and a single team responsible for every phase, from site preparation through final landscaping coordination and your certificate of occupancy.

Our Build Process

A clear, proven process from first conversation to final walkthrough, so you always know what comes next.

  1. 01

    Site Evaluation

    We assess the land, utilities, access, and any site challenges before a shovel goes in the ground.

  2. 02

    Design & Planning

    We collaborate with you and your architect to finalize plans, materials, and a detailed budget.

  3. 03

    Permitting

    We handle all permit applications with local Berkshire County municipalities, no delays on your end.

  4. 04

    Build Phase

    Our crew executes every phase on schedule, with regular client updates and open-book transparency.

  5. 05

    Final Walkthrough

    We walk every inch with you, complete any punch list items, and hand over the keys.
