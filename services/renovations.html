Our Approach

The Old Bones.
A New Life.

Berkshire County is full of exceptional older homes — Victorian farmhouses, Craftsman cottages, mid-century colonials. These homes have character that no new build can replicate. They also have decades of deferred maintenance, inefficient systems, and layouts that no longer match how people live.

Keating Builders specializes in whole-home renovations that honor a home's original character while fully modernizing its systems, efficiency, and livability. We're experienced with the structural quirks of older construction, lead paint and asbestos abatement protocols, historic district requirements, and the patience required to do this work right.

Whether you need a full gut renovation or a phased modernization over several years, we work as your single point of contact for every trade. One plan, one budget, one team accountable to you.

How We Work

Our Renovation Process

A methodical, phased approach that keeps a large renovation manageable — and keeps you informed throughout.

  1. 1

    Home Assessment

    We walk every inch of the home — structure, systems, and surfaces — to understand what's there before we plan what comes next.

  2. 2

    Scope of Work

    A detailed written scope with itemized costs — no vague line items, no "we'll figure it out later" surprises.

  3. 3

    Phased Planning

    We sequence the work intelligently — structural first, then systems, then finishes — minimizing disruption and protecting completed work.

  4. 4

    Build

    Our crew executes each phase on schedule, with regular client updates and clear communication about any conditions uncovered.

  5. 5

    Inspection

    All required inspections passed, punch list completed, and a final walkthrough before we consider the project closed.
