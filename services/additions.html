Our Approach

Expand Your Home. Not Your Stress.

A home addition is one of the most technically demanding projects in residential construction. Done well, new square footage looks and feels like it was always part of the original home. Done poorly, it looks exactly like what it is: a box tacked onto the side of a house.

Keating Builders builds additions that integrate fully: matching the roofline, siding, trim profiles, flooring, and interior character of your existing home. We coordinate with architects when needed, handle all permit applications with local municipalities, and manage the full build from foundation to final trim.

We build bump-outs, full two-story additions, garage apartments, sunrooms, and everything in between. If you've outgrown your home but love where you live, an addition is often the best investment you can make, and we know how to make it count.

Our Addition Process

Six phases from initial feasibility through seamless final integration with your existing home.

  1. 01

    Feasibility Study

    We evaluate your lot, setbacks, zoning, and structural connection points before any design work begins.

  2. 02

    Design

    Working with your architect or ours, we develop plans that integrate naturally with your existing home's style and structure.

  3. 03

    Permitting

    We file and manage all permit applications with Berkshire County municipalities, start to approval.

  4. 04

    Foundation

    Excavation, foundation, and structural framing set the stage for everything that follows, done right the first time.

  5. 05

    Build

    Framing, roofline, exterior, systems, and all interior finishes, matched precisely to your existing home.

  6. 06

    Integration

    We connect the addition to the rest of your home, flooring, trim, paint and systems, so the seam disappears.
