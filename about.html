Our story

Your neighbors. Your builders.

Keating Builders was founded right here in Berkshire County, not in a corporate boardroom but around a kitchen table. We started with one crew, one guiding principle, and a deep respect for the homes and landscapes that make this corner of Massachusetts so special.

Over three decades later, we're still family-owned, still locally focused, and still putting our name on every project the same way we always have: with care, skill, and an honest commitment to getting it right. We've built new homes from the ground up and carefully restored century-old farmhouses. We've transformed outdated kitchens into gathering places and turned dated bathrooms into true retreats.

What hasn't changed is our belief that every homeowner deserves a builder they can trust: one who shows up on time, communicates clearly, and takes as much pride in your home as you do.

The Keating Builders commitment

Three things we hold ourselves to on every project, large or small.

  • Craftsmanship first

    We don't cut corners, ever. Every joint, every finish, every detail is executed to the standard we'd want in our own homes. We take on fewer projects specifically so we can do each one exceptionally well.

  • Transparent pricing

    You'll receive a detailed written quote before a single nail is driven. We explain every line item, answer every question, and stand behind our numbers. No hidden fees. No surprise change orders after the contract is signed.

  • On-time delivery

    Your home is your life, not just a job site. We plan meticulously, communicate proactively, and respect your schedule and your space. When we commit to a timeline, we mean it.

How we work

From the first phone call to the final handshake, our process is clear, collaborative, and as stress-free as a major construction project can be.

  1. 01

    Free consultation

    We start with a no-pressure conversation, at your home or over the phone, to understand your vision, your timeline, and your goals. No commitment required, ever.

  2. 02

    Detailed quote

    You receive a full written estimate that breaks down materials, labor, and timeline, so you know exactly what you're getting and what it costs before anything begins.

  3. 03

    Expert build

    Our skilled crew gets to work. We keep your home clean and secure, communicate updates regularly, and hold every subcontractor to our own high standards throughout the build.

  4. 04

    Final walkthrough

    Before we consider a project complete, we walk through every inch with you. Any item on your punch list gets addressed promptly and without complaint. Your satisfaction is the finish line.
